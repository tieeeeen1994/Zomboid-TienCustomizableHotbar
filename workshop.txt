title=Tien's Customizable Hotbar
description=A second hotbar where any item you carry can go, even one deep in a bag. Press its slot and the item comes out of the bag into your hands; press again and it goes back where it came from. The game's own hotbar gets the same handle and buttons, so you can move it anywhere and put its slots in the order you like.
description=
description=[h2]The Customizable Hotbar[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Any item you carry:[/b] click [i]+[/i] and pick from your inventory and every bag, drag an item from the inventory window onto the bar, or right-click it > [i]Add to Customizable Hotbar[/i]. No belt or holster needed. Up to 40 slots.
description=[*][b]Out of the bag, into your hands:[/b] the slot takes the item out of its bag (at the game's usual transfer speed, so a rifle at the bottom of a hiking bag is slower than a knife on your belt) and equips it. Press again and it is put away and goes back into that bag.
description=[*][b]Switching:[/b] with the gun out, press the knife's slot: the gun goes back into its bag, then the knife comes out.
description=[*][b]Clothing and bags[/b] are worn and taken off instead of held.
description=[*][b]Your item first:[/b] a slot remembers the exact item you put on it. If it isn't on you, another item of the same kind is used; with none on you the slot is faded out.
description=[*][b]Any action from the item's menu:[/b] right-click a slot > [i]Action[/i] to drink, take pills, turn on, wear, or anything else the item's own menu offers.
description=[*][b]Your keys:[/b] Options > Key Bindings > Tien's Customizable Hotbar has 20 slot keys, none bound at first. Shift, Ctrl and Alt combinations work. Clicking a slot always works.
description=[*][b]Looks like the game's hotbar:[/b] same frame, slots, numbers, hover labels and item tooltips. Hovering a slot says which bag the item is in and where it goes back to.
description=[*][b]Settings:[/b] size (Small, Normal, Large), item names under the slots, vertical, reset position.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Both hotbars[/h2]
description=Each bar has a dotted handle at its left end and four small buttons at its right end. Hover a button to see what it does.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Move it:[/b] drag the bar by its dotted handle. It stays where you put it.
description=[*][b]Reorder the slots:[/b] drag a slot to another place on the bar. A green line or frame shows where it will go. A click without dragging still uses the slot.
description=[*][b]Lock:[/b] the bar and its slots stay where they are.
description=[*][b]Swap / insert:[/b] dropping a slot either slides it in between and shifts the others, or trades places with the slot it lands on.
description=[*][b]Eye:[/b] hides the Customizable Hotbar (its keys keep working). The eye on the game's hotbar brings it back.
description=[*][b]Gear:[/b] the bar's settings.
description=[*][b]Snap together:[/b] drag one bar near the other and it docks flush against it, then follows it around. Drag it away to undock; hold Shift to move without snapping.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]The game's hotbar[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Reordered slots stay that way:[/b] the number keys follow the new order, and the order is kept when you log out and back in, in single player and on a server.
description=[*]Everything else works as before: use a slot, drop an item on it, right-click it for its menu.
description=[*]Replaces [b]Reorder The Hotbar[/b]: don't use both.
description=[/list]
description=
description=Build 42 only. It uses the game's own timed actions, so it works in multiplayer like the vanilla hotbar. Slots are saved with the character; the bars' look, positions and buttons on your computer.
